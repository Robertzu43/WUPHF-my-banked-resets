A little louder. A lot less forgetful.

Your banked Codex resets deserve a WUPHF!

WUPHF is an open-source desktop app that checks your saved Codex resets and helps remind you before they expire.

Explore the project ↗Read our privacy policy

How it works

  1. Connect Codex. WUPHF reads your signed-in Codex CLI’s reset information on your computer.
  2. Choose how to hear from us. Configure reminders through Gmail, other email, Telegram or ntfy; export calendar events if you like.
  3. Keep your computer awake. WUPHF checks in the background and reminds you on your chosen schedule.

WUPHF is an early macOS prototype. Gmail and Google Calendar connections are being tested; availability and delivery are not guaranteed.

Google permissions

If you connect Gmail, WUPHF asks for permission to send reminder emails on your behalf. It does not request permission to read your Gmail messages. The optional Google Calendar feature requests separate access for a WUPHF-created calendar.
