Your data, your desktop

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

WUPHF! for Codex Banked Resets is an independent, open-source desktop reminder app. This policy describes the current macOS prototype and its optional integrations.

Data WUPHF uses

WUPHF reads reset counts, identifiers and expiry information from the locally signed-in Codex CLI. It stores settings, reset identifiers, reminder history, errors and channel configuration locally on your Mac so it can schedule and deduplicate reminders. Email addresses and notification destinations you enter are stored locally. Credentials and Google refresh tokens are stored in macOS Keychain.

Google user data

If you choose to connect Gmail, WUPHF requests the gmail.send permission to send reminder and test emails from your account to the address you configure. It does not use that permission to read your inbox. Message content and the recipient are sent to Google’s Gmail API to deliver the message. WUPHF stores the authorization refresh token in your Mac’s Keychain and exchanges it with Google when needed.

If you separately connect Google Calendar, WUPHF requests access to create and manage its own secondary calendar and sends reset expiry information to Google to create or update events. Calendar events may remain after a reset is used or expires; remove outdated events manually. A downloaded calendar file is a snapshot and is not updated automatically.

WUPHF’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

If you enable Telegram, ntfy, or SMTP email, WUPHF sends the reminder text and destination information to the provider or server you configure. Those providers handle the data under their own policies. Public ntfy topics can be read by anyone who knows the topic name. WUPHF does not operate a central reminder server.

Retention and removal

Local settings and reminder history remain until you delete them. Use the app’s disconnect controls to remove stored Google credentials, and revoke WUPHF’s access in your Google Account if you want to withdraw authorization. Disconnecting locally does not delete previously created Google Calendar events. To remove local data, follow the uninstall instructions in the project README and remove WUPHF entries from macOS Keychain.
